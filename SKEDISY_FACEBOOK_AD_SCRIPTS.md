## Canva Tips

1. **Templates:** Use "Facebook Video Ad" or "Instagram Reels" in Canva.
2. **Screens:** Use screenshots from the app (home, booking, payment, confirmation).
3. **Text:** Large, bold font; keep on screen 2–3 seconds minimum.
4. **Music:** Choose upbeat, light background music (15–30 sec loop).
5. **Brand colors:** Use Skedisy’s black/green palette for consistency.
6. **Export:** MP4, 1080x1920 (vertical) or 1080x1080 (square).
