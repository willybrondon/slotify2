## Description d'activité — **anglais** (Revolut)

**Version courte (1 ligne)**

```
Digital platform connecting customers with hair salons and beauty service providers in France.
```

**Version détaillée**

```
Skedisy operates a digital marketplace (website and mobile apps) that connects customers with independent hair salons, particularly specialized in Afro hair care, in France. The company provides online booking, appointment management for professionals, salon visibility, and digital tools such as service recommendations and visual look sharing. Skedisy earns revenue through platform fees, subscriptions, and/or commissions on bookings. The company does not perform hairdressing or beauty treatments directly; it acts as a technology platform operator partnering with independent salons.
```

**Version ultra-courte (si limite de caractères)**

```
Digital platform connecting users with service providers (online booking for hair & beauty salons in France).
```

## Description d'activité — **français** (statuts / cohérence)

```
Conception, développement et exploitation d'une plateforme numérique (site web et applications mobiles) de mise en relation entre clientes et salons de coiffure, notamment spécialisés en coiffure afro, en France. Service de réservation en ligne, gestion des créneaux et visibilité des salons partenaires. Rémunération par intermédiation et services numériques (abonnements, commissions, frais de plateforme). Aucune prestation de coiffure réalisée directement par la société.
```
