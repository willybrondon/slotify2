Frequently Asked Questions

How to create a salon owner account?

  1. Download the Skedisy Customer App and sign up as a customer first.
  2. Go to the Profile section and select Register your salon.
  3. Fill in your salon details and specify the number of experts (staff) you want to add.
  4. Once approved, you will receive credentials for your expert(s).

How does an expert get their credentials?

The salon owner creates expert accounts during salon registration. Each expert receives their login credentials from the salon owner.

What is the difference between the Customer and Expert app?

  • Customer App: For clients booking at your salon.
  • Expert App: For your team to manage bookings and schedules.

How can a salon owner add a new expert, add services, and manage their business on the business owner platform?

Log in to Skedisy Salon Panel to manage experts, services, bookings, and your salon profile.

My salon is already listed — how do I claim it?

Use the claim link sent by email or visit skedisy.com/salon/claim to take ownership of your profile.
