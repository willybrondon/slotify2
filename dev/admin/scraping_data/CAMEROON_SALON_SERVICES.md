# Salon Services in Cameroon

This document lists the different services commonly offered in salons, spas, and beauty centers across Cameroon, particularly in Yaoundé and Douala.

## Overview

Cameroon's beauty industry offers a diverse range of services catering to both traditional and modern beauty needs. The market is heavily influenced by:
- **Wig culture**: High demand for wig installation and customization
- **Afro hair styling**: Specialized services for natural African hair
- **Nail art**: Creative nail designs and extensions
- **Spa and wellness**: Growing demand for relaxation and therapeutic services

## Service Statistics

### Most Popular Services in Cameroon

1. **Wig Installation** - Very high demand, especially lace front wigs
2. **Hair Braiding (Tissage)** - Traditional and modern styles
3. **Hair Extensions** - Peruvian, Indian, Malaysian hair
4. **Microblading** - Growing popularity for eyebrows
5. **Nail Art** - Creative designs
6. **Gel/Acrylic Nails** - Long-lasting nail extensions

### Service Duration Range
- **Quick services**: 15-30 minutes (beard trim, eyebrow shaping)
- **Standard services**: 45-90 minutes (manicure, facials)
- **Extended services**: 120-240 minutes (braiding, extensions, wig installation)
- **All-day services**: 300+ minutes (dreadlocks)

## Pricing Notes

- Prices vary significantly by salon location and quality
- **Yaoundé & Douala** centers generally have higher prices
- Most salons accept cash payments (CFA francs)
- Some upscale salons accept credit cards
- Advance booking is recommended, especially for:
  - Wedding services
  - Wig installation
  - Extended braiding/weaving services
