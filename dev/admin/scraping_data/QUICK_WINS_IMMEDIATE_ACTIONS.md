## ✅ **STEP 2: Design Physical Marketing Materials** (2-3 hours)

### Option A: Use Canva (Free/Easy)
1. **QR Code Poster**:
   - Size: A4 (21 x 29.7 cm)
   - Design: "Scan to Book Your Appointment"
   - Include: QR code (you already have the functionality)
   - Add: Skedisy logo, app download instructions
   - **Template**: Search "QR code poster" in Canva

2. **Mirror Sticker**:
   - Size: Small (10 x 10 cm)
   - Design: "Book your next visit with Skedisy!"
   - Include: QR code
   - **Template**: Search "sticker design" in Canva

3. **Table Card**:
   - Size: A6 (10.5 x 14.8 cm)
   - Design: "Book online and get 10% off your first booking!"
   - Include: QR code, app download link
   - **Template**: Search "table card" in Canva

### How to Get QR Codes:
1. Go to any salon page: `https://skedisy.com/salon/{salon-slug}`
2. Click share button
3. Generate QR code
4. Screenshot or download QR code
5. Add to Canva designs

**Total Time**: 2-3 hours
**Cost**: Free (Canva free plan) or $12.99/month (Canva Pro)
**Result**: Ready-to-print marketing materials

## ✅ **STEP 3: Create Salon Integration Guides** (1 hour)

### Guide 1: "How to Add Skedisy Link to Instagram Bio"
**Create a simple PDF or webpage with screenshots:**

1. Open Instagram app
2. Go to your profile
3. Tap "Edit Profile"
4. Tap "Website" or "Link in Bio"
5. Paste your Skedisy salon link: `https://skedisy.com/salon/{your-salon-slug}`
6. Save

**Include**: Screenshots for each step

### Guide 2: "How to Add Skedisy Link to Facebook Page"
1. Go to your Facebook Page
2. Click "Edit Page Info"
3. Scroll to "Website"
4. Add: `https://skedisy.com/salon/{your-salon-slug}`
5. Save

### Guide 3: "How to Add Skedisy Link to Google Business Profile"
1. Go to Google Business Profile
2. Click "Edit Profile"
3. Add website: `https://skedisy.com/salon/{your-salon-slug}`
4. Save

**Total Time**: 1 hour
**Result**: 3 guides ready to send to salons

## ✅ **STEP 6: Set Up First Ad Campaign** (1 hour)

### Instagram Ad (5-10€/day budget)

1. **Go to**: Facebook Ads Manager (ads.facebook.com)
2. **Create Campaign**: "Traffic" or "App Installs"
3. **Targeting**:
   - Location: Paris, France (or your city)
   - Age: 18-45
   - Interests: Beauty, Hair salons, Nail salons
   - Gender: All (or focus on women if that's your target)
4. **Budget**: 5-10€/day

6. **Duration**: 7 days

## ✅ **STEP 8: Create First Booking Discount Coupon** (15 minutes)

### In Admin Dashboard:

1. **Go to**: Admin Dashboard → Coupons
2. **Create New Coupon**:
   - Code: `FIRST10` or `WELCOME10`
   - Title: "10% Off First Booking"
   - Description: "Get 10% off your first booking on Skedisy"
   - Discount Type: Percentage (10%)
   - Max Discount: 10€ (or your preferred amount)
   - Min Amount: 0€
   - Expiry Date: Set to 1 year from now
   - Type: Appointment
   - Active: Yes

3. **Share Code**: Add to all marketing materials

**Total Time**: 15 minutes
**Result**: First booking discount coupon ready
