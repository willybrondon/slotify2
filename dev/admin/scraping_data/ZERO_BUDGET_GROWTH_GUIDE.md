## 📱 **INSTAGRAM GROWTH STRATEGY ($0 Budget)**

### **1. Content Pillars (Post 3-5x per week)**

#### **A. Transformation Content (40% of posts)**
- **Before/After Haircuts**: Quick 15-30 second Reels
- **Nail Art Timelapses**: Show the process
- **Makeup Transformations**: Dramatic changes
- **Hair Color Changes**: Bold transformations
- **Use trending sounds**: Check Instagram Reels trending page daily

**Example Post Ideas:**
- "3-second booking, 30-minute transformation ✨"
- "From booking to beautiful in one tap 📱"
- "This salon booked through Skedisy 👆"

#### **B. App Demo Content (30% of posts)**
- **Screen Recordings**: "How to book in 3 seconds"
- **Feature Highlights**: Show AI Concierge, instant booking
- **User Testimonials**: Real booking experiences
- **Quick Tips**: "5 ways to find the perfect salon"

**Example Post Ideas:**
- "Book your next appointment faster than ordering coffee ☕"
- "No more phone calls, no more waiting 📞❌"
- "Your salon, your time, your way ⏰"

#### **C. Behind-the-Scenes (20% of posts)**
- **Salon Tours**: Partner with salons
- **Stylist Spotlights**: Feature expert stylists
- **Day-in-the-Life**: Show salon operations
- **Team Content**: Show the Skedisy team

#### **D. Educational Content (10% of posts)**
- **"How to choose the right salon"**
- **"What to expect on your first visit"**
- **"Booking tips and tricks"**
- **"Cancel/reschedule guide"**

### **2. Instagram Growth Tactics**

#### **Hashtag Strategy (Use 20-30 hashtags per post)**
**Mix of:**
- **Broad hashtags** (1M+ posts): `#beauty #hair #nails #makeup #salon`
- **Medium hashtags** (100K-1M posts): `#haircut #nailart #beautysalon #haircolor`
- **Niche hashtags** (10K-100K posts): `#salonbooking #beautyapp #onlinebooking #bookappointment`
- **Location hashtags**: `#parisbeauty #frenchsalon #parishair`
- **Brand hashtag**: `#skedisy #skedisyapp #bookwithskedisy`

**Hashtag Research:**
- Check competitor hashtags
- Use Instagram's search suggestions
- Monitor trending hashtags daily

#### **Engagement Strategy**
1. **Post at Optimal Times**: 9 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM (local time)
2. **Respond to ALL Comments**: Within 1 hour if possible
3. **Engage with Competitors**: Like and comment on similar accounts
4. **Follow Beauty Influencers**: Engage with their content
5. **Use Instagram Stories Daily**: Behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&A
6. **Create Reels**: Instagram prioritizes Reels in algorithm

#### **Collaboration Strategy (Free)**
1. **Micro-Influencers (1K-10K followers)**:
   - Offer free salon bookings in exchange for posts
   - Feature them on your account
   - Create "Salon of the Week" feature

2. **Salon Partnerships**:
   - Ask salons to tag @skedisy in their posts
   - Create co-branded content
   - Feature salons in your content

3. **User-Generated Content (UGC)**:
   - Create hashtag: `#SkedisyTransformation`
   - Repost user content (with permission)
   - Run monthly "Best Transformation" contest

#### **Instagram Features to Use**
- **Reels**: 15-30 seconds, use trending sounds
- **Stories**: Daily updates, polls, Q&A
- **IGTV**: Longer tutorials (5-10 minutes)
- **Carousel Posts**: Multiple images showing booking process
- **Link in Bio**: Direct to app download or salon booking

### **3. Instagram Content Calendar (Weekly)**

**Monday**: Transformation Reel  
**Tuesday**: App Feature Highlight  
**Wednesday**: Salon Partner Spotlight  
**Thursday**: User Testimonial  
**Friday**: Weekend Booking Reminder
