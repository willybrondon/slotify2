# Social Media Marketing Email Address Guide

## Recommended Email Addresses for Social Media Accounts

For creating TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram marketing accounts, here are the recommended email addresses:

### ✅ **Best Option: `marketing@skedisy.com`**

**Why this is the best choice:**
- ✅ **Clear purpose** - Immediately identifies it as marketing-related
- ✅ **Professional** - Shows organized business structure
- ✅ **Scalable** - Can be shared by marketing team
- ✅ **Separate from support** - Keeps marketing inquiries separate from customer support
- ✅ **Easy to remember** - Standard format users expect

### Alternative Options (Ranked)

#### 1. `marketing@skedisy.com` ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- Best for: All social media accounts
- Use for: TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X
- Pros: Clear purpose, professional, scalable

#### 2. `hello@skedisy.com`
- Best for: Friendly, approachable brand image
- Use for: Instagram, TikTok (more casual platforms)
- Pros: Friendly tone, easy to remember
- Cons: Less specific, might receive general inquiries

#### 3. `contact@skedisy.com`
- Best for: General contact point
- Use for: All platforms
- Pros: Standard business email
- Cons: Too generic, might mix with other inquiries

#### 4. `info@skedisy.com`
- Best for: Information requests
- Use for: Facebook Business Page
- Pros: Standard format
- Cons: Very generic

#### 5. `social@skedisy.com`
- Best for: Social media specific
- Use for: All social platforms
- Pros: Very specific to social media
- Cons: Less common, might confuse some users

### ❌ **NOT Recommended: `support@skedisy.com`**

**Why NOT to use support@skedisy.com:**
- ❌ **Confusing** - Support emails should be for customer service, not marketing
- ❌ **Mixed purposes** - Marketing and support should be separate
- ❌ **Poor organization** - Hard to filter and manage emails
- ❌ **Unprofessional** - Shows lack of organization

```

marketing@skedisy.com  → Marketing & social media
hello@skedisy.com      → General inquiries (optional)
```

- Monitor for spam/phishing attempts
