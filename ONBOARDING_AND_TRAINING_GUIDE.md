### 3.3 Managing Your Availability

#### A. Attendance Marking
**Daily Attendance:**
- Mark yourself as available/unavailable at the start of your shift
- Attendance dialog appears on first app open
- Yes = Available for bookings
- No = Not available (blocks your bookings for the day)

**Importance:**
- System will not show you for bookings if marked unavailable
- Helps salon manage customer expectations
- Tracked for performance reviews

#### B. Slot Management
**Setting Your Availability:**
- View your weekly schedule
- Configure time slots (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)
- Morning slots: 6 AM - 12 PM
- Afternoon slots: 12 PM - 5 PM
- Evening slots: 5 PM - 10 PM

**Managing Slots:**
- Mark specific time slots as available/unavailable
- Set busy periods for breaks or personal time
- Update availability in real-time
- Block entire days for leaves/holidays

**Best Practices:**
- Update slots at least 24 hours in advance
- Keep slots updated during holidays
- Inform salon manager of extended unavailability

### 3.4 Booking Management

2. Select reason for cancellation

**Important Notes:**
- Must check-in before check-out
- Cannot check-out without checking in first
- Cancellations impact your rating

### 4.3 Booking Appointments

#### A. Selecting a Salon
**Salon Details Page:**

- Services offered with pricing
- Expert/staff profiles
- Customer reviews and ratings
- Working hours
- Contact information (Call button)
- Location and directions

**About Section:**
- Salon description
- Specializations
- Years in business
- Unique features

#### B. Booking Process (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1: Select Services**
1. Browse available services
2. Tap on service to view details
3. Tap "Add" to add to booking
4. Add multiple services if needed
5. View subtotal
6. Tap "Continue" or "Book Now"

**Service Details:**
- Service name and description
- Duration (in minutes)
- Price
- Service category
- Available experts

**Step 2: Choose Your Expert**
1. View available experts/staff
2. See expert photos and bios
3. Check expert ratings
4. Select preferred expert
5. OR select "Any Available Expert"
6. Tap "Next"

**Expert Information:**
- Experience level
- Specializations
- Average rating
- Number of reviews
- Photo

**Step 3: Select Date & Time**
1. View calendar
2. Select your preferred date
3. View available time slots
4. Slots shown by time of day:
   - Morning (6 AM - 12 PM)
   - Afternoon (12 PM - 5 PM)
   - Evening (5 PM - 10 PM)
5. Select available slot (unavailable slots are grayed out)
6. Tap "Next"

**Slot Availability:**
- Green = Available
- Gray = Not Available
- Shows expert availability
- Real-time updates

**Step 4: Review & Confirm**
1. Review booking details:
   - Salon name and location
   - Selected services
   - Total duration
   - Expert name
   - Date and time
   - Subtotal
   - Tax (if applicable)
   - Total amount
2. Confirm all details are correct
3. Select payment method

**Step 5: Payment**
**Payment Options:**
- Wallet (if you have balance)
- Credit/Debit Card
- UPI/Digital Wallets
- Cash on arrival (if enabled)

**Making Payment:**
1. Select payment method
2. Enter payment details (if card)
3. Tap "Confirm Booking"
4. Wait for confirmation
5. Booking successful!

**After Booking:**
- Receive booking confirmation
- Get booking ID
- Receive SMS/Email confirmation
- See booking in "My Bookings"
- Receive reminder before appointment
